Relational Safety & Respect

What Working Together Actually Looks Like

Therapy should feel collaborative, safe, and deeply respectful of your autonomy.

01.

No Cold Clinical Jargon or Diagnoses

I am not here to stick labels on you or treat you like a case file. You are a whole person carrying real, understandable pain. We speak plainly, human to human.

Human to human presence
02.

You Control the Pace and the Depths

We never force you to recount traumatic memories before you feel ready. Trust is earned slowly. If on some days you just need to talk about your week or sit in quiet contemplation, that is completely okay.

At your own comfort
03.

A Space Where All Feelings Are Welcome

Anger, sadness, numbness, confusion, or even humour — nothing you bring into the cabin is ‘too much’ or ‘wrong’. You don't have to put on a brave face here.

Unconditional acceptance

Therapeutic Philosophy

The Soulful & Creative Approach

Blending deep listening, metaphorical insight, and gentle expressive media.

Why “Soulful”?

Inspired by Thomas Moore's Care of the Soul, I see emotional struggles not as broken machinery to fix, but as the soul calling for attention.

When you describe feeling ‘stuck in a fog’, ‘heavy as lead’, or ‘at the bottom of a well’, those images carry wisdom. We listen to what the sadness or anxiety is trying to tell you.

Depth Psychology · Thomas Moore · James Hillman

Creative Expression in the Cabin

Sometimes talk therapy reaches an intellectual wall. In the cabin, I keep paper, pastels, paints, and stones. You never have to be ‘artistic’ — simply sketching an emotion or choosing a colour can unlock insights that words couldn't reach.

“The soul doesn't speak in logical arguments; it speaks in images, longings, and feelings.”
No artistic background required — solely an open, non-judgmental invitation.
