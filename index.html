About Caroline Vieira, Integrative Transpersonal Therapist

Therapy that listens to the soul, not just the symptoms

With over two decades in the field, I practise integrative, transpersonal therapy that blends conventional therapeutic thinking with creative approaches, visualisation, drawing and enactment, so you can include all of yourself, safely and with care.

"The care of the soul adds depth and meaning to everyday life, with heart, warmth, connections, intelligence and vision."
— Thomas Moore

I am inspired by Thomas Moore and James Hillman's soulful perspective. The soul speaks in images: you may say you feel 'in a fog', 'bogged down', or like you're 'at the bottom of a well'. We pay attention to those images, and to dreams, because that is where your inner life shows itself. And if words are hard, you don't have to use them, I keep paper, paints and colours in the studio... Above all, my work rests on relationality: the way we meet and relate is where we meet ourselves, and where healing takes place.
